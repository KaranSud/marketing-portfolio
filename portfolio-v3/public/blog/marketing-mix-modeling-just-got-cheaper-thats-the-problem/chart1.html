More teams running MMM. Same teams measuring wrong.

Adoption is surging while confidence and coverage remain low — IAB State of Data 2026, TransUnion/eMarketer 2026

The adoption paradox: Nearly half of brand and agency marketers plan to invest in MMM, yet three in four say their measurement models underperform. Access to the tool is not the same as knowing how to use it.

Sources: IAB State of Data 2026 (n=400+); TransUnion/eMarketer 2026 survey (n=marketers, U.S.)
