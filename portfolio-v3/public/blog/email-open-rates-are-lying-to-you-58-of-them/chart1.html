Average Email Open Rate, 2019–2025
Industry average across all verticals. Bars in red reflect post-MPP inflation from Apple's server-side pixel pre-fetching.

Apple MPP launched Sept 2021. Within six months, industry open rates rose 18 percentage points — not because audiences became more interested in marketing email, but because Apple's servers began firing tracking pixels automatically on email delivery.

Sources: Litmus Email Client Market Share (2025); MailerLite Benchmark Report; multiple ESP industry benchmarks. Pre-2021 rates reflect genuine engagement signals; post-2021 rates are substantially inflated by machine-generated opens.
