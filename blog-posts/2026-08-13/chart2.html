Only 33% of what your dashboard calls "opens" are genuine human reads. The remaining 67% are machine-generated signals from Apple's privacy system, Google's image proxy, and security infrastructure. Teams making A/B, suppression, and re-engagement decisions on these numbers are optimizing for server behavior, not subscriber behavior.

Sources: Litmus Email Client Market Share Report (Dec 2025); Validity State of Email 2025; multiple deliverability research reports. Percentages are estimates for an iOS-heavy consumer list and will vary by audience composition.
