1/ Human-written content earns 5.44x more traffic per post than AI-generated content. B2B teams tripled their AI content output in 2025. The math doesn't work. Here's why:

2/ NP Digital tracked 744 articles across 68 websites for 5 months. By month 5: human posts averaged 283 visits. AI posts averaged 52. The gap widened every single month.

3/ The AI content didn't just underperform. It didn't compound. Month 3 was worse than month 1. Human content built trajectory. AI content fluctuated flat.

4/ Meanwhile, 91% of B2B marketers increased content output in 2025. Nearly half tripled their volume. Most received budget increases of less than 10%. Everyone scaled the same direction.

5/ When everyone publishes more AI content simultaneously, the volume advantage disappears. Publishing 20 posts/month means nothing when your entire category does the same.

6/ This is the content volume trap: AI made production cheap, so teams optimized for production. But the bottleneck was never production. It was always editorial judgment.

7/ McKinsey estimates AI can unlock $463B in annual marketing productivity. Most teams are cashing it on the wrong dimension — more output instead of better signal.

8/ The real productivity gain: using AI to accelerate research and structure, then applying human judgment at the stage that creates differentiated output. Angle. Evidence. Opinion.

9/ Gartner called a 25% search volume drop by 2026. The actual US drop was ~2.5%. But the structural shift is real: AI Overviews now appear on 48% of queries, dropping CTR to 8%.

10/ Here's the irony: AI answer engines favor content that is demonstrably human-sourced. Proprietary data. Named experts. Specific case evidence. Temporal specificity.

11/ The overlap between top-10 Google rankings and AI Overview citations collapsed from 75% in mid-2025 to 17-38% by early 2026. Ranking and citation are now two separate games.

12/ What actually earns citations: original data from your own research. Specific experience from real work. A stake in the ground that creates friction with conventional wisdom.

13/ For a team of 5: publish 8 pieces/month instead of 25. Make 4 of them irreplaceable. Proprietary benchmark, named author's specific read, or original survey data.

14/ NP Digital's 2025 report: 56% of marketers saw traffic increase post-AI Overviews. The ones gaining aren't publishing more. They're publishing harder.
