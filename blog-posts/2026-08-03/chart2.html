Human Content vs. AI Content: Monthly Traffic Per Post

Average page visits per published post over 5 months — 744 articles across 68 websites

By month 5, human-written posts averaged 283 visits vs. 52 visits for AI-generated posts. The gap widened every month.

Source: NP Digital, "AI vs. Human Content" study — 744 articles, 68 websites, 5-month tracking period (neilpatel.com/blog/ai-vs-human-content)
