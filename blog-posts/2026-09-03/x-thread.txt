1/ 67% of B2B buyers prefer a rep-free experience. They decided before your SDR sent a single email. (Gartner, March 2026, n=646)

2/ The playbook most B2B SaaS teams are running was built for a world where buyers searched Google and you intercepted them. That world is gone.

3/ Today's buyer asks a Pavilion Slack channel. Runs a query through Claude or ChatGPT. Notices who gets mentioned by practitioners they respect. Then arrives at your website with a shortlist already formed.

4/ McKinsey B2B Pulse: buyers now use 10 interaction channels across their journey. Up from 5 in 2016. Almost none of those channels are in your marketing dashboard.

5/ The dollar amounts involved are not small. 39% of B2B buyers now complete $500K+ purchases through self-serve digital channels. That is up from 28% just two years ago. (McKinsey, 2024)

6/ Forrester called it in 2024: more than half of $1M+ B2B transactions would move through digital self-serve channels by 2025. (investor.forrester.com) That is enterprise sales, not SMB.

7/ The 5 places where your shortlist is actually built: peer Slack communities, AI research (ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity), G2 and review platforms, dark social (LinkedIn DMs, private shares), executive/founder content.

8/ 72% of Gong's website traffic is direct or dark. 71.6% of HubSpot's is the same. (Similarweb) That traffic came from somewhere. It was not Google Ads.

9/ What winning looks like now: original research that gets cited, community participation under real names, systematic review platform coverage, consistent founder POV content. Slow. Compounds.

10/ The counterintuitive part: Gartner also says by 2030, 75% of buyers will prefer human interaction at the closing stage. Digital wins early. Humans win late. Your tactics need to work in both registers.

11/ The companies losing are pouring more budget into demand capture. More paid search, more outbound sequences, more SDR reps. They're fighting for the 33% of buyers still in an open search.

12/ The companies winning are treating pre-funnel presence as infrastructure, not brand awareness. They publish consistently. They show up in communities. They measure branded search growth, not just MQL volume.

13/ A practical starting point: audit your G2 presence today. Build a process to surface happy customers to review platforms. Commission original research from your customer base. Pick two communities and show up weekly.

14/ Results are not immediate. The entire point is that they shouldn't be. Dark funnel presence compounds over 6-12 months into the kind of reputation that puts you on every buyer's Day One shortlist.
