1/ The platforms are not failing you. You are failing the platforms.

Most B2B marketers blame Smart Bidding and Advantage+ for bad results. The algorithm is fine. The signal you gave it is broken.

Thread on why — and how to fix it.

2/ A B2B SaaS company running Target CPA against form fills is asking Google to find more people who fill out forms.

Google oblige. It finds students, researchers, consultants, and tire kickers.

Cost per form fill drops. Pipeline does not grow. The algorithm worked exactly as designed.

3/ The scale of this matters.

U.S. AI-powered ad spend hit $57B in 2026, up 63% YoY. Meta's Advantage+ alone crossed $75B annual run rate.

Nearly 10M advertisers now use at least one AI creative tool. Most are training their models on the wrong signal.

4/ Smart Bidding requires 15 conversions in 30 days to exit learning mode. Stable optimization needs ~50/month.

Most B2B SaaS accounts never produce 50 qualified pipeline leads from paid search. So teams default to form fills. That is where the problem starts.

5/ Every conversion event is a vote.

Form fill votes say: find more people who look like this.

In a 90-day sales cycle, the whitepaper downloader and the Series B CFO requesting a demo look identical to Smart Bidding. It has no way to distinguish them. So it optimizes for form fillers.

6/ Accounts that run form fill optimization for 2+ quarters have typically trained their model to avoid high intent audiences.

Their best buyers behave differently from the form fill population. The algorithm has learned to pass on them. Every single auction.

7/ There are 4 signal tiers for B2B AI bidding:

T1: Form fill only (62% of accounts)
T2: Form fill + delayed MQL upload (19%)
T3: CRM-connected pipeline event, real time (14%)
T4: Offline closed-won with revenue value (5%)

The gap between T1 and T4 is not a platform difference. It is an input difference.

8/ Signal debt compounds.

Every week on bad signals = another week reinforcing the wrong audience profile.

Accounts that ran form fill optimization for 18 months face a 60-90 day recovery window after switching to pipeline signals. Performance drops before it recovers.

9/ The fix is sequenced, not instant.

1. Find highest quality event you can generate at 30+/month
2. Set as primary optimization target
3. Add downstream CRM event as secondary with value
4. Run both in parallel for 30 days minimum
5. Only then switch primary target

That 30-day window is not optional.

10/ Three things to do before scaling another AI campaign:

First: Audit your primary conversion event. Trace 90 days of Smart Bidding conversions into your CRM. If under 5% became sales opps, you are optimizing on noise.

11/ Second: Connect your CRM.

Google Enhanced Conversions + Offline Conversion Upload. Meta Conversions API. Both accept pipeline data. Most major CRMs have native integrations.

Two to four hours of dev work. The return routinely outweighs months of creative testing.

12/ Third: Sequence before you scale.

Adding budget to a poorly signaled campaign does not fix the signal. It funds the wrong audience at greater volume.

Fix the signal. Validate for one full learning cycle. Then scale.

13/ The top quartile of B2B advertisers using value based bidding with real time CRM signals see results that look unfair.

They are not using a different platform. They are using a different input. The algorithm is the same for everyone.

14/ With global ad spend above $1 trillion and AI buying growing at 63% per year, every advertiser running clean CRM signals is improving their model while yours stagnates.

The question is not whether to fix your signal architecture. It is how much debt you can afford to accumulate first.

15/ The algorithm has never been the bottleneck.

Your definition of success always has been.
