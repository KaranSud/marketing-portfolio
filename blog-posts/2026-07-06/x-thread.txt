X THREAD: Marketing Attribution Created the Doom Loop
Published: 2026-07-06
Post: karansud.substack.com

---

1/14
84% of companies are stuck in what Gartner calls the "brand doom loop."

Marketing can't prove its value. Budget gets cut. Measurement gets harder. Value gets harder to prove.

The cycle feeds itself.

What nobody says out loud: last-touch attribution built this loop.

Thread.

---

2/14
Here is the doom loop in four moves:

1. Can't measure brand value
2. Shift budget to attributable channels (search, retargeting)
3. Those channels get expensive because they capture existing demand, not new demand
4. CFO asks for ROI story. CMO has no good answer.
5. Budget gets cut. Return to step 1.

---

3/14
The measurement gap is real.

Only 52% of senior marketing leaders successfully prove the value of their department and get credit for it.

That is from a Gartner survey of 378 CMOs and marketing leaders, published in September 2024.

Half of the people running major marketing orgs can't defend what they spend.

---

4/14
Why can't they prove it?

Attribution software answers: "Which channel recorded the last interaction before a conversion?"

The CFO is asking: "Did your marketing spending cause revenue to grow?"

Those are not the same question. The tools are measuring something real. They're just measuring the wrong thing.

---

5/14
The signal in the budget data is hard to miss.

62.6% of total media spend now goes to awareness and conversion channels (up more than 10% since 2024).

Loyalty and retention spend dropped 29% in the same period.

Source: Gartner 2026 CMO Spend Survey, 401 CMOs.

Last-touch attribution is doing exactly what it was built to do.

---

6/14
The standard industry response: buy better attribution software.

Multi-touch attribution. Customer data platforms. Revenue attribution modelers with machine learning.

None of it fixes the real problem.

64% of B2B marketing leaders don't trust their own measurement data for decision-making. Not "don't have it." Don't trust it.

(Forrester, 2024)

---

7/14
"Ninety percent of attribution tech is theater."

That is Pranav Piyush, CEO of Paramark, the measurement startup that raised $8M in 2025.

The dashboards are real. The data pipeline is real. The insight about what actually caused revenue growth is, in most cases, not there.

---

8/14
The real cost of broken measurement: $26.3 billion in wasted media investment value that improvements in measurement could release.

Plus $6.2 billion in industry-wide productivity.

That is the IAB's estimate from the State of Data 2026 report, based on 400+ senior planning and analytics leaders.

---

9/14
The path out is not a better attribution model.

It is a measurement philosophy that can answer the causal question:

Not "which channel appeared last?"

But "did this investment cause revenue to grow that would not have grown otherwise?"

Three tools exist to answer it.

---

10/14
Tool 1: Self-reported attribution.

Add one question to every lead form: "How did you first hear about us?"

The answer will not match your CRM. That gap is the measurement gap.

Refine Labs has data showing software-based attribution misses 93% of LinkedIn-influenced pipeline. LinkedIn drives early-stage awareness, not last clicks.

---

11/14
Tool 2: Incrementality testing.

Instead of "which channel gets credit," ask "what would have happened if we hadn't run this?"

Run a geo-holdout test. Compare regions where you ran the campaign vs. regions you paused.

The revenue difference is the actual incremental value. Causal proof, not modeled proxy.

This is how Google and Meta validate their own ad products internally.

---

12/14
Tool 3: Marketing mix modeling (MMM).

Google open-sourced Meridian. Meta open-sourced Robyn. Both free.

MMM uses historical spend and revenue data to model channel contribution over time.

It cannot tell you which individual clicked what. It CAN tell you whether your brand spend is producing revenue growth. That is the CFO's actual question.

---

13/14
Gartner's 2026 prediction is unusually specific:

By 2027, 40%+ of CMOs who push for larger brand budgets will lose C-suite influence because they cannot demonstrate sufficient returns.

That is not a budget prediction. That is a career prediction.

The companies in the doom loop are already 2x less likely to exceed their growth targets.

---

14/14
The 90-day escape plan:

Week 1: Add "how did you first hear about us?" to every lead form. Free. No IT required.

Weeks 4-10: Run one geo-holdout test in one channel. Six weeks. Measure the revenue difference.

Month 3: Run a basic MMM model with 2 years of spend data using Meridian or Robyn. Show the CFO.

The loop breaks the moment you stop asking which channel closed the deal and start asking which investments created the demand.
