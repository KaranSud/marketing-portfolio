1/ Forrester 2026: the average B2B SaaS purchase involves 13 internal stakeholders + 9 external influencers.

Most marketing funnels are built for 1 of them.

That's the 92% problem. Thread on what it costs you and how to fix it.

2/ If your strategy is: build a persona → gate content → score an MQL → hand off to sales...

You're influencing 1 of 13 people before the deal enters your pipeline.

The other 12 are forming opinions in private Slack channels, analyst calls, and G2 reviews.

3/ Procurement now participates in 53% of B2B buying cycles.

Most SaaS marketing teams have produced zero content for procurement.

No TCO docs. No vendor risk frameworks. Nothing that speaks to their actual job.

Then deals die in legal review and nobody knows why.

4/ The timing problem makes this worse.

6sense 2024 (n=4,000): buyers are 61% through a 10.1-month journey before they contact a vendor.

Your first MQL doesn't start the journey. It lands two-thirds of the way through it.

5/ What's happening in those 6 months before first contact?

The 13-person committee is comparing vendors, sharing articles, checking reviews, and forming opinions about whether your company feels credible.

Before a single contact enters your CRM.

6/ McKinsey 2026 B2B Pulse (n=4,000, 13 countries):

60% of market leaders reported double-digit revenue growth.
21% of laggards did.

The gap isn't budget. It's architecture. Leaders market to the room. Laggards market to the contact record.

7/ The AI marketing budget surge is making the problem worse, not better.

15.3% of CMO budgets now go to AI tools (Gartner 2026, n=401).

Most of that is making the same single-persona funnel faster. Faster content. Faster email. Faster MQL scoring.

You can't automate your way out of marketing to the wrong unit.

8/ Fix 1: Expand the content unit from persona to committee role.

Procurement needs TCO models and vendor risk frameworks.
Legal needs compliance docs.
IT needs integration specs.

Without these, your champion is selling internally with assets built for external consumption.

9/ Fix 2: Invest in dark funnel channels.

G2 review management. LinkedIn content your champion can forward without it looking like a sales pitch. Analyst brief programs. Peer communities.

These don't generate MQL attribution. They generate the conditions under which a champion can close a committee.

10/ Fix 3: Change the measurement unit from MQL to account activation (MQA).

An MQL = 1 person crossed a threshold.
An MQA = multiple roles at a target account engaging simultaneously.

That predicts pipeline velocity better than any single-contact score.

11/ Gartner August 2025: by 2030, 75% of B2B buyers will prefer human interaction over AI.

Right now 67% prefer rep-free. That preference inverts over the next 4 years.

The companies building committee-level authority now are setting up for that inversion.

12/ These things compound slowly. Committee relationships, analyst credibility, community presence, peer reputation.

They cannot be turned on in a quarter when demand drops.

The room has been deciding without you for months. The question is whether you're part of the conversation.
