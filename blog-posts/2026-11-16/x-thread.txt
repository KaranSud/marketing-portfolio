1/ The 2.6% freemium conversion rate isn't a goal.

It's a confession that most PLG teams are optimizing the wrong stage of the funnel.

Here's the upstream problem nobody talks about 🧵

2/ Most teams spend quarters on:
- Pricing page copy tests
- Email drip sequences
- Conversion flows

Then the needle doesn't move.

The free-to-paid number stays at 2-3% and nobody knows why.

3/ The honest answer: you can't fix conversion when activation is broken.

Activation is not "user completes onboarding."

It's the moment a new user FIRST experiences the core value your product was built to deliver.

4/ Proxy metrics that don't predict conversion:
❌ Onboarding checklist complete
❌ 3 logins in first week
❌ Email verified

What actually matters: did they reach the value moment?

5/ The 5x gap hiding in your trial data:

Free trial (CC required): ~30% conversion
Free trial (no CC): 5-8%
Freemium: 2.6%

Most people read this as "CC = intent."

The real mechanism is structural.

6/ A time-limited trial with a CC forces the team to define the activation event.

You have 14 days to prove value. That pressure builds better products.

Freemium products rarely have that clarity — and their conversion rates show it.

7/ OpenView's 2025 Product Benchmarks: median B2B freemium-to-paid conversion = 2.6%

ProductLed survey of 600+ B2B SaaS companies: only 34% track activation rate as a formal metric.

Two-thirds of PLG companies don't know their activation rate.

8/ This is the most expensive measurement gap in product-led SaaS.

Without activation rate, there's no leading indicator for conversion.

Your first signal is a low number at month end — after those users already left.

9/ PQL conversion rates run 25-30%.
MQL conversion: 5-10%.

That gap isn't magic. It's what happens when a company actually knows who has activated.

The 2.6% median reflects what happens when they don't.

10/ McKinsey studied 107 publicly listed PLG companies.

Average PLG company: no meaningful growth premium over sales-led peers.

Top decile PLG executors: +10pp ARR growth, ~50% higher valuation ratios.

The differentiator? Precision on product usage signals.

11/ What the best PLG teams do differently:

→ Define activation in a cross-functional session (product + marketing + CS)
→ Set time-to-value target from their best cohort, not the average
→ Build a secondary trigger that fires when activated users don't convert

12/ The single operational change that moves the needle:

Replace "free-to-paid conversion rate" as your primary metric with "activation-to-paid conversion rate."

Keep tracking the first. Make the second your signal.

13/ When you track activation-to-paid:
- You force a definition of the activation event
- Marketing optimizes for activated users, not signups
- Sales knows exactly which accounts to touch and when

The aggregate 2.6% stays — but inside it, your activation-to-paid might be 18-25%.

14/ Every percentage point you add to your activation rate is worth more than a full campaign budget rerouted to free-tier acquisition.

The data has been saying so for two years.
