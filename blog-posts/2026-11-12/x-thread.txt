1/
By the time your SDR sequence fires, the buyer has already decided if you're on their shortlist.

6sense surveyed 4,000 B2B buyers. First vendor contact happens at 61% of the journey.

The ranking is done before you're in the room.

2/
Most B2B SaaS marketing tactics are built for hand-raisers -- form fills, demo requests, ad clicks.

Those are lagging indicators. The actual decision happened weeks, sometimes months, earlier.

The buying process runs in two phases. Standard SaaS marketing misses both.

3/
Phase 1 is rep-free.

Gartner (March 2026): 67% of B2B buyers prefer a rep-free buying experience.

A separate Gartner survey found 45% of those same buyers used AI tools during a recent purchase.

4/
Phase 2 is human -- but not for discovery.

Gartner (May 2026): 69% of B2B buyers turn to sales reps to validate AI-generated insights.

The buyer already has an answer. They want confirmation, not education.

5/
Put those two data points together:

Phase 1 = AI and peer sources build the shortlist (rep-free)
Phase 2 = human rep confirms what AI said (validation, not discovery)

This is the actual map of a B2B deal in 2026.

6/
The timing makes this urgent.

The winning vendor was already ranked #1 on the buyer's initial shortlist in 80% of deals (6sense).

Competing at the demo stage is not where wins are determined.

7/
The shortlist itself is shrinking.

Average vendors evaluated per deal: ~3.2 a few years ago. Now ~2.5.

Fewer chances to make the list at all. And AI is compressing the research phase, not extending it.

8/
The phase-one problem = your content strategy has a second audience.

ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity -- these tools are synthesizing vendor comparisons before buyers ever visit your website.

If you're not in those answers, you're not on the shortlist.

9/
I call this the day-zero layer.

It's the signals and presence that exist before a buyer fills a form:
- AI-readable content
- G2 reviews in ICP language
- Genuine community presence in the 2-3 Slacks where your buyers ask vendor questions

10/
None of this shows up in standard attribution.

That's a feature, not a bug. It means your competitors aren't tracking it either.

11/
The rep's job is shifting.

It's not shrinking -- it's being repositioned from early-stage education to late-stage confirmation.

Gartner predicts: by 2030, 75% of B2B buyers will prefer sales experiences that prioritize human interaction over AI.

The human moment moves later. It doesn't disappear.

12/
Phase-two prep requires new inputs:

Run regular AI audit queries. Search for your company in ChatGPT and Perplexity with ICP-phrased questions.

What AI says is what your buyer's priors look like before the call. Marketing can fix inaccuracies before the rep walks in.

13/
Metrics for phase-one performance:

- Branded search volume (buyers who first heard of you via AI then searched directly)
- Direct traffic share from ICP accounts
- Demo-to-close win rate on first-touch-unknown deals vs. campaign-sourced

None are in most SaaS dashboards. All are more predictive than MQL volume.

14/
The teams that win won't have the best demos.

They'll be the ones whose names appear in the right AI answers at the moment nobody is watching.
