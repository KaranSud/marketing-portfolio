# portfolio v4 plan: "Flow" (25 Oct 2026, for Karan's review)

## 1. Audit

### Why the last three concepts failed
The feed, control room and rewind were all **containers**: a fake app, a dashboard, a slider. You click into
them, and the page itself never moves. Karan wants a page that moves *with* you as you scroll, the way his two
references feel alive. Lesson: the motion has to be the story, not a frame around the story.

### anushriya.com (checked in a browser, 25 Oct)
- One personal line leads: "I'm an engineer turned designer. I understood systems before design." Her
  background IS the positioning. That is exactly the line Karan wants for himself.
- Warm paper page that subtly "breathes"; serif headlines (Fraunces) with a clean sans body.
- Hero facts in three short columns (Currently / Previously / Fellowship), a photo and a friendly note.
- Work = one big case card with 3 numbers and a **live animated mini-demo** of the product, not a screenshot.
- A playground of tiny interactive toys (poke an eye, a pixel canvas). Serif article cards.
- Footer flips to a dark, twinkling night sky with the email in giant type. "You and 2 others are here right now".
- Its life comes from many small moving details (rise, pop, float, twinkle), never from scroll-jacking.

### frvky.online (checked 25 Oct)
- The interface is the story (lock screen, then desktop); every artefact inside is real; testimonials arrive as
  notifications; playful discovery. Lesson: one strong idea, executed all the way through.

### Karan's live v2 and current v3
- v2 has the approved logo marquee (round white logo chips with names); it must be reused exactly (CLAUDE.md rule).
- v3 already has an authentic gem buried in the hero: a real **CFD flow field** (streamlines bending around your
  cursor, potential flow past a cylinder). It is the only thing on the site that comes from Karan's own story.
- v3's problems: dark and heavy, text first, the work small, the engineer story hidden in one small line.

## 2. The concept: "Flow"
**Karan used to simulate how air flows. Now he makes attention flow.**
The whole site is one continuous flow simulation that carries the visitor from his engineering past to his
marketing work. The streamlines in the hero are the same lines that carry his posts down the page. It is
dynamic by nature (it never stops moving), it is his real story, and nobody else's portfolio can copy it.

Look: warm paper white (like anushriya), ink text, one sage accent (#6CA088, already his). **No client brand
colours** in the design; colour comes only from the posts themselves. As you scroll, the page slowly shifts from
paper to a night palette, so the footer arrives dark, the way anushriya's does.

## 3. The page, top to bottom
1. **Hero: "Engineer turned marketer."** Large serif headline on paper. Under it: "I studied mechanical
   engineering and worked in CFD, simulating how air flows. In June 2023 I moved into marketing. Now I make
   attention flow to brands and founders." Live CFD streamlines fill the background and bend around the cursor
   (or a finger). Three fact columns: *Works with* brands and founders, project or part-time / *Previously*
   FanTV AI, Crowwd Labs, Mach1 Digital / *Based in* Noida. Photo plus "Book a free 30 minute call".
2. **The pivot (signature moment).** On scroll, the hero's streamlines straighten and turn into lanes; real posts
   ride them in from the edge like particles in a wind tunnel, faster where the flow is faster. A label rides
   along: "Same equations. Different fluid."
3. **Logos.** The v2 marquee exactly as it is (two rows drifting in opposite directions, round white logo chips
   with names).
4. **The work, as flowing streams.** One stream per client: a continuous ribbon of their 20 to 25 posts drifting
   sideways at its own speed (pauses on hover, drag to scrub, tap to open large; reels play). Beside each stream:
   name, role and dates, one or two verified numbers that count up as they enter. Streams alternate direction,
   so the page feels like layered currents. "Read the case" opens the full case.
5. **How I work, as a pipe network.** Four nodes (Content, Reach, Trust, Revenue) joined by a pipe that fills as
   you scroll; each node lights up with one real post and one number.
6. **What clients say.** Quotes arrive like notifications sliding in (the 4 testimonials when Karan has them).
7. **Things I built (the engineer's playground).** The account research engine and SlouchGuard as small live
   toys, plus the flow field itself: "Play with the simulation".
8. **Writing.** Serif article cards from his blog.
9. **Footer at night.** The streamlines become faint stars; the email in giant type; "Book a free call".

## 4. Motion system
- One easing curve, cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1); everything rises, never pops in.
- Canvas2D flow field (already built, reused and extended), CSS scroll-driven animation and IntersectionObserver
  for reveals; Framer Motion (already installed) for the post streams and counters. No smooth-scroll library
  (it broke the checks last time). Native scroll, nothing hijacked.
- 60fps budget: canvas pauses off screen; streams use transform only; reduced motion gets still frames.
- Phones: the same story, simpler (fewer particles, swipeable streams, 44px taps, tested at 375/414/768).

## 5. Rules carried in
- Logos exactly as v2. No brand colours. Lead with engineer turned marketer. Brands and founders, not AI only.
- Every number from facts.md. 20 to 25 posts per client, only strong, creative, well performing ones; reels play.
- Never name a current employer. No prices. Free call is the one action.

## 6. Build order (each step something you can look at)
1. Hero with the flow field on paper + the pivot moment + logo marquee. (Review: does it feel alive?)
2. One client stream (FanTV, 24 posts already prepared) + counters + large view.
3. Pull posts for every other client (scripts are ready), then all streams.
4. Pipe network, testimonials slot, playground, writing, night footer.
5. Case pages restyled to match. Checks (tsc, build, lint, taste inspector, phone check), then a Vercel preview link.
