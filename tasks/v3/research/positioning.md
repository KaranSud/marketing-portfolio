# Positioning research for portfolio-v3

Lane: positioning. Written 2026-10-15. Sources fetched the same day; every fetch and search is in `research-log.md`.
All Karan numbers below are Karan-provided (`portfolio-v2/lib/caseStudies.ts`) and wait on `research/facts.md` before they go on the site.

## Decision

Position Karan as the growth and content marketer for AI products, with crypto-native AI as his deepest proof and his
own working software as the mechanism that sets him apart. One page serves freelance and full-time buyers because both
buy the same thing (users for an AI product) and read the same proof; they only split at the last step.

Recommended hero (draft 1 below): "You built the AI product. I find the people who use it."

Why, in one paragraph: the benchmarks that convert name a narrow buyer and a concrete problem (Kaleigh Moore, Blueprint
GTM, Eli Schwartz), and Karan's rarest proof is usage, not reach: daily active traders, signups and trading volume on
AI and crypto products (caseStudies.ts:159-161, 208, 250). Almost no content marketer leads with usage numbers. The
crypto marketer field has no polished personal sites at all (log 20:28 to 20:30), so the bar there is low.

Three findings that matter most for Karan:
1. Narrow beats broad. Kaleigh Moore and Eli Schwartz both sell one thing to one buyer. Live v2 lists 12 service cards
   across seven channels and four industries (`portfolio-v2/components/Services.tsx:6-51`), which is the weak pattern.
2. "Not X, Y" slogan heroes cluster in the weak group (8, 10, 12). v2's hero, "Brands don't go viral. Systems do."
   (`portfolio-v2/components/KineticHeadline.tsx:9-10`), is the same construction. Retire it.
3. The two AI-GTM operators (5, 6) prove the method by showing outputs. Karan's live Account Research Engine
   (https://account-research-five.vercel.app) is that kind of proof; buyers in the Clay community say they value the
   full path from ICP to accounts to outreach more than clever workflows (log 20:28).

## 3. Karan's positioning

products, ideally in AI", and requests for case studies with revenue impact. Ryan's head-of-content notes name the same
buyer: founders, CMOs and heads of growth at early-stage AI, crypto, DTC and F&B startups
(`~/.claude/skills/head-of-content/references/hoc-article-ryan.md`, "What it means for Karan" item 1).

|---|---|---|---|

Recommendation: A, with B as the mechanism. A has his strongest and rarest numbers, matches the buyer who hires both
fractional and full-time (AI startups hiring a first growth or content lead), and stays true to his two biggest case
studies. B is what passes the logo-swap test: plenty of marketers claim AI products, few ship working software, and
the engineering degree explains why he can. C stays on the site as range (F&B and founder branding cases in the
archive), never in the hero.

How one site serves freelance and full-time without muddling:
- Same buyer, same proof, same story. Founders hiring a contractor and managers hiring a lead both read case studies
  with numbers; the job posts above ask for exactly that.
- Split only at the ask. One primary CTA ("Work with me") opens two clear doors: a scoped project, or a full-time role.
  Eli Schwartz's "Hire me" menu (row 7) and MKT1's separate advising address (row 4) are the precedent. The conversion
  lane owns the mechanics.
- One availability line near the CTA, written as fact Karan confirms, e.g. which project slots and which full-time
  titles. No "open to work" banner and no résumé timeline on the homepage.
- Cut services to three that map to the outcome (content, community, launch and paid campaigns). The other nine v2
  cards become case-study tags.

NEEDS KARAN before any of this ships: (1) is he employed full-time now (Cyberwave note in BRIEF), and can the site say
he is open to roles; (2) which full-time titles he wants (his profile reportedly lists Brand Manager, Creative
Strategist, Marketing Manager per the hoc notes above, which is a different ladder from "growth lead"); (3) whether he
will take crypto-only clients or wants crypto shown as context only; (4) what his role was when Fere AI hit 7K+ daily
active traders.

## 4. Hero lines for option A

Brief (head-of-content Stage 1, condensed). Buyer: founder of a seed-stage AI product with a working build and few
active users, or the manager hiring that company's first growth lead. Job: bottom of funnel; the one belief is "he has
already done the job I need." Reframe: judge marketing by people who use the product. Only Karan: usage numbers from AI
and crypto products plus software he built himself. Sophistication: late, so it needs a mechanism and named proof.
Next step for the reader: case studies, then the two-door CTA. Score: form submissions tagged project or full-time.

Every number is Karan-provided and must clear `facts.md`. Attribution wording keeps to what the role supports.

1. You built the AI product. I find the people who use it.
   Sub: Growth and content marketing for AI and crypto teams, as a project or as your full-time hire.
   Note: lived-moment opener that makes the buyer the protagonist; numbers go in the proof strip right under it.

2. Growth for AI products, counted in daily users.
   Sub: Fere AI reached 7K+ daily active traders and 12K+ signups while I managed its content at Crowwd Labs.
   Note: strongest proof-first line; only works if case studies also lead with usage metrics. Needs NEEDS KARAN item 4.

3. I get AI products used.
   Sub: My work includes FanTV AI, where X followers grew from 70K to 300K and the community from under 1K to 150K.
   Note: shortest; the subhead does all the credibility work.

4. A growth marketer who ships his own software.
   Sub: I market AI and crypto products, and I build working tools, like the live account research engine below.
   Note: best logo-swap score; risk is that it sells the builder over the outcome, so use it as the section that
   introduces the tools if draft 1 or 2 wins the hero.

5. The growth and content lead for AI products.
   Sub: Past work with Fere AI, FanTV AI and Novaswap, and open to projects or a full-time role.
   Note: Eli Schwartz-style label; plain and safe for hiring managers, weakest at stopping a founder.

Pick: draft 1 for the hero, draft 4 as the heading of the tools section, draft 2's subhead as the first proof line.

Checks run: lessons.md rules by hand and `tasks/v3/workflows/copy-lint.sh tasks/v3/research/positioning.md`
