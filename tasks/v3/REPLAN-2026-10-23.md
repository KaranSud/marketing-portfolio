## 2. Strategy (landing page skill, Part A)
- **One offer, one audience, one action.** Founders of AI products who need growth and content help, as a project or
  part-time. One action everywhere: **Book a 30-minute call**. No second CTA above the fold.
- **Layout type A, classic hero plus sections.** Why: the work explains itself in one hero visual (a wall of real posts),
  so the page does not need a long story to educate. Case pages use type B (long form) for the visitors who want depth.
- **Proof next to the claim.** Every number sits beside the post or screenshot that backs it.
- **Risk reversal (needs your OK):** "The call is free. Pricing comes after it, once we agree the scope." The second
  sentence is your own answer from 16 Oct; the first I have not confirmed with you.

## 5. Visual system
- **Type:** Geist only, regular to semibold (no black weights, no italics). Display text-8xl/9xl on desktop,
  text-5xl/6xl on phones; body text-base/text-lg; buttons text-base semibold.
- **Colour:** flat #181818 base, #1F1F1F and #272727 surfaces, off white text, one accent (your sage #6CA088).
  The posts bring the colour; the page stays quiet around them. No background gradients. Subtle grain overlay for depth.
- **Motion:** one curve everywhere, cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1), 700ms; sections fade up from a soft blur
  (800ms) as they enter, via IntersectionObserver; the hero wall drifts; reels snap with CSS scroll-snap;
  the large post view zooms out of its tile. Reduced motion respected.
- **States:** hover, pressed, focus ring, skeleton loading for the post walls, real empty and error states.
- **Performance with 250+ images:** small WebP or AVIF thumbnails, full size only in the large view, lazy loading,
  reserved space so nothing jumps. Target under 2.5s to first paint on a phone.
