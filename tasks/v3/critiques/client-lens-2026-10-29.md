# Client-lens review, 29 Oct 2026 (a founder or brand lead who lands on the site cold)

## What works
- The first screen says who he is and why he is different in two seconds: "Hi, I'm Karan Sud. Engineer turned
  marketer." The live airflow makes the CFD story felt, not just told.
- The logo strip and the ten streams of real posts show volume and range fast; reels playing in place beat screenshots.
- Every number has a source and a date range; nothing reads as inflated. One clear action everywhere (free call).

## What a client stumbled on (fixed today)
1. "What can you do for me?" was not answered until the FAQ. Added "What I can do for you": four services, each
   with the client proof under it (content and social, founder brands, community and launches, ads).
2. Ten client chapters in a row: a restaurant owner had to scroll past five AI and crypto brands. Added "Jump to the
   work closest to yours", grouped by industry, linking to each client.
3. Rolling numbers read as "2 9 7 . 7 K" to search engines and screen readers. Each number now carries its full
   value as hidden text.
