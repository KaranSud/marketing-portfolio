Where Search Referral Traffic Still Comes From (2026)

Share of all web search referral traffic by source — Cloudflare Radar, April 2026

All AI chatbots combined (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity) account for just 0.27% of search referrals — roughly one-fifth the share of DuckDuckGo alone. Google's 87.5% hold remained stable through Q3 2026.

Source: Cloudflare Radar (April 2026, 28-day window)
