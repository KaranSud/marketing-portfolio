PLG Metrics: How Many Teams Track Them vs. How Well They Predict Revenue

Radar showing the gap between tracking adoption (% of PLG companies measuring this metric) and its predictive power for free-to-paid conversion

The measurement inversion: The metrics PLG teams track least (Time-to-Value at 17%, PQL signals at 24%) are the ones with the highest predictive power for revenue. Signups and MQLs — tracked by nearly everyone — are the weakest predictors of conversion.
Sources: ProductLed 2025 Annual Benchmarks Survey; OpenView Partners Product Benchmarks 2023–2025; High Alpha 2025 SaaS Benchmarks. Revenue prediction power = correlation with free-to-paid conversion rate, normalized 0–100.
